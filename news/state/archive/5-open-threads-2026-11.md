## Threads opened 1 Nov 2026
- **A state act, mostly paid for.** The FBI named TraderTraitor for Binance's US$6.3bn; Binance replaced ~90 per cent from company funds and set aside ~US$6.34bn internally. Watch whether withdrawals reopen as promised, whether any indictment or sanctions action follows the attribution, and whether exchange custody is moved into regulated territory.
- **An order with an expiry date.** Judge Thompson's TRO bars DOJ and DHS from coercing election officials until 16 November; DOJ appealed within a day. Watch whether the Ninth Circuit leaves it in force through 3 November, whether the DHS data-matching lists survive the other cases examining them, and whether any official is charged after it lapses.
- **A revolt with a shared calendar.** 320,000 marched in France on a third date, a new "Gen Z regelt" movement filled four German cities, and Italy joined. Watch whether a fourth coordinated date is called, whether conscription becomes the German movement's central demand, and whether any government concedes on cost-of-living measures rather than policing.
- **Targets without a treasury.** COP17's Yerevan Pathway makes revised national biodiversity plans conditional on available funding and defers the post-2030 finance mechanism to criteria only. Watch whether China or another wealthy emerging economy accepts donor status, what the GEF and GBFF reviews conclude, and whether the US$500bn subsidy-cut target gets a single national schedule.
- **An apology offered, reparations refused.** Burnham will hear calls for a formal state apology for the slave trade but rules out payments, before the Antigua CHOGM. Watch whether the summit communiqué records an apology, what "practical help" is quantified as, and whether CARICOM's 15 states accept the split.
- **Compliance by persuasion.** Five haze-noticed companies agreed to engage assessors, Kencana Agri is willing and First Resources has said so publicly; Bumitama Agri, TSH Global Plantation and TSH Oversea have not. Watch whether the last three agree or force NEA to use the Act's powers, and whether self-appointed assessors produce findings anyone treats as conclusive.
- **Half the island covered.** Project Wolbachia reached 840,000 households (53 per cent) in its tenth year, with Tampines down from ~1,200 dengue cases in 2020 to 77 in 2025. Watch whether DENV-3's rise in Q3 2026 breaks the suppression record, and whether NEA commits to coverage beyond 53 per cent.
- **Nine years of reasoning, released at once.** The Employment Claims Tribunal published 17 judgments in 2026, its first since 2017, including a maximum S$30,000 award for a dismissal at the end of probation. Watch whether claim volumes rise, whether the S$30,000 cap is revisited, and whether employers change probation documentation.
- **The oldest town densified.** Toa Payoh West is to take more than 6,000 homes, the town's first mall, a 1.2ha wellness park and possibly a school, starting with the 1,600-unit Toa Payoh Hillside BTO in November. Watch the November launch's application rates, whether the school is confirmed, and what the mall does to Toa Payoh Central.
- **Care moved closer, weekly.** The three clusters upgraded 35 community health posts in 2026, NHG's 27 going monthly to weekly, with 12 more by March 2027. Watch whether utilisation justifies the frequency, whether frailty screening at AACs feeds referrals, and whether the model is costed against avoided admissions.
- **Carried, not run (1 Nov):** Poland's tribunal crisis in its second day — riot police deployed, Tusk defending the overhaul in a prime-time address, Nawrocki squaring off (incremental on 31 Oct; the one to run if charges are filed or the Court of Justice is asked to rule). Judge Stearns blocking the CMS "medically frail" definition under the Medicaid work requirement for 25 states and DC, decided 29 Oct and reported just outside the window. Ethiopia's 12 Russian Su-35s and four S-400 batteries, announced 28 Oct, against renewed Tigray fighting. DR Congo's Ebola outbreak past 5,000 deaths and 10,000 cases. Also held: Syria weighing accession to the Saudi-Turkey-Pakistan mutual defence pact; the completed Oleshky evacuation and reunions; Erin Patterson resentenced to life; Spain's traffic-control attribution and judge-led inquiry for the Urda collision; the Nasdaq's worst month in over a year; Edwin Tong's ICJ remarks (overlapping 31 Oct's Oceans Four story, with the 3 November ICJ vote on Rena Lee still pending); the record S$1.818m Queenstown five-room resale; HDB's review of the jumbo-flat conversion scheme; and rising bone-marrow donor registrations against only ~20 per cent of 500 annual searches ending in a transplant.

- **No-repeat log (covered 1 Nov):** The FBI's TraderTraitor attribution and Binance replacing ~90 per cent of the US$6.3bn; Judge Thompson's TRO against DOJ and DHS pressure on election officials and the Saturday appeal; the 320,000-strong third French mobilisation with "Gen Z regelt" in Germany and protests in Italy; COP17's Yerevan Pathway and the deferred post-2030 finance mechanism; Burnham's openness to a slavery apology with reparations ruled out before CHOGM; five haze-noticed companies agreeing to engage assessors with Kencana and First Resources signalling; Project Wolbachia passing 53 per cent of households in its tenth year; the Employment Claims Tribunal's first 17 published judgments and the S$30,000 award; the Toa Payoh West masterplan and the 1,600-unit Toa Payoh Hillside BTO; and 35 community health posts upgraded in Singapore's first super-aged year.

## Threads opened 2 Nov 2026
- **A bargain with a stopwatch.** Qatar has compressed Iran's Hormuz plan to 48 hours — US measures on day one, reopening on day two — and is brokering more than US$6bn of the ~US$12bn held in Doha. Watch whether Washington answers before 3 November, whether the money actually moves, and whether any reopening is independently verified.
- **A revolt the state can file as extremism.** Hesse blames ~100 identified left-wing extremists for Frankfurt's looting; France has opened an attempted-murder case over petrol bombs at riot police. Watch whether the infiltration account becomes official, whether a fourth coordinated date is called across the five countries, and whether conscription stays in the German demand list.
- **Both pauses ended on the same day.** The Kherson truce expired at 22:59 on 1 Nov and Kyiv resumed refinery strikes against an explicit US request, drawing a Russian terrorism case. Watch whether humanitarian measures are extended, whether Trump imposes any cost on Kyiv, and whether Russian refining throughput falls measurably.
- **Pre-emptive certification, nought for two.** Judge Gleason dismissed the DOJ's bid to block Alaska's Senate result, three days after Judge Thompson's TRO; both are on appeal. Watch what the Ninth Circuit does before Tuesday, whether the Alaska ballot-plot inquiry charges anyone, and whether the instrument is tried in a third state.
- **A roll nobody has audited.** The INDIA bloc filled Ramlila Maidan demanding CEC Gyanesh Kumar resign over the Special Intensive Revision, with Rahul Gandhi putting deletions at ~13.5 crore. Watch whether an independent audit of the deletions is ever produced, whether the impeachment motion is filed, and whether the Commission publishes a reconciliation.
- **A spill with no owner.** MPA confirmed oil patches off Pulau Sebarok, Pulau Semakau and Selat Pauh on 1 Nov and activated its contractors with the source unidentified. Watch whether a vessel or terminal is named, whether shorelines are affected, and who ends up paying for the cleanup.
- **One number, one point of failure.** From 2 Nov every police call comes from 6000-0999 with a gov.sg verification SMS, after government-impersonation losses hit S$142.5m in nine months against S$61.4m a year earlier. Watch whether case volumes fall, whether other agencies follow, and whether scammers learn to spoof the new line.
- **Top-up cover being shed at scale.** 181,800 Integrated Shield riders were dropped or downgraded in 2025, against 103,400 in 2024, with MOH saying it does not track reasons. Watch whether 2026 figures accelerate, whether MOH starts collecting reasons, and whether out-of-pocket claims rise at the restructured hospitals.
- **The Gulf as a precedent for Malacca.** Zaqy Mohamad told the 22nd Manama Dialogue that restricting transit through Hormuz or Bab el-Mandeb could erode navigational rights in the Straits of Malacca and Singapore, citing the IFC's 58 partners and 163 linkages. Watch whether any Hormuz settlement records a transit-rights principle, and whether the IFC's partner count is converted into escort capability.
- **Procedure over the reparations question.** DPM Gan Kim Yong leads Singapore's delegation to CHOGM in Antigua, 2-5 November, with London having agreed structured dialogue on reparatory justice while keeping it off the formal agenda. Watch what the communiqué records, and whether the summit produces any trade or standards outcome for small states.

The G20 Atlanta family photo scrapped over Lavrov (30 Oct). Netanyahu's coalition arithmetic and the Arab-led parties' 19 seats before 4 November certification. Syria weighing accession to the Saudi-Turkey-Pakistan pact. Poland's tribunal crisis with Tusk's prime-time defence. The UK's 10-year settlement rules (announcement due 2 Nov). London's structured CHOGM dialogue on reparatory justice, folded into the Gan story. The WSJ/Epstein dismissal. Senator Chris Murphy's stroke. Nine dead in a Palm Beach County Learjet crash. Kimi Antonelli, F1 champion at 20. Also held: the 1,310-flat Sembawang November BTO launch (near-repeat of 1 Nov's Toa Payoh Hillside item); the government's build-to-rent study (announced 3 Aug, today's coverage was explanatory); at least 20 schools hiring external manpower for student discipline ahead of the 2027 standardised measures; the ST decoy-research figures, which could not be grounded; and the Istana Open House's first inclusive-employment showcase.
