## Lessons not yet folded into the rules
Working lessons taken from changelog entries, kept here so they are not lost when those entries are archived. One line each, with the dates of the entries they come from. When an entry about to be archived holds a lesson that still applies and is not already in `news/AGENT.md` or `2-format-rules.md`, add it here in one line. Remove a line once the owner folds it into the rules or it stops being true. Keep the list to about ten lines.
- **Feeds work; most article pages do not.** Plan on RSS plus search. BBC is feed-and-search only (the proxy blocks its article hosts). Guardian, Straits Times, CNA and lite.cnn.com pages usually come through by plain `curl` with a desktop user-agent, and WebFetch is blocked for many hosts, so prefer `curl` plus a local text extractor. The Guardian challenges a second fetch of the same URL in one run, so read each article once. Straits Times, CNA and lite.cnn.com article pages still come through by `curl`; the Guardian now challenges most article fetches even one at a time, and WebFetch is refused for it. (2 Sep, 25-26 Sep, 6 Oct, 21 Oct, 3-4 Nov, 8-9 Nov)
- **Web search lags same-day events.** When a feed carries an in-window item that search cannot find, trust the feed and fetch the article. Take same-day figures from fetched articles, not search summaries. (1 Nov, 3-4 Nov)
- **Mothership's feed is not well-formed XML.** Extract `<item>` titles and dates by regex, not an XML parser. Its timestamps carry no offset and are SGT (+08:00). A parse error there is not a dead feed. (2 Oct, 31 Oct, 2-6 Nov)
- **The PMO newsroom often returns an empty page.** Record the cabinet check as negative from feeds plus search rather than skipping it. (2 Sep, 21 Oct, 5 Nov)
- **If `git push` is rejected with `commit_refs`,** publish the report through the GitHub API, then `git fetch`, rebase onto the API commits and push again. Do not conclude publication is impossible. The large state files cannot go through the API tools inline. (6 Nov)
- **Convert the window to US time before judging a US story in-window.** 0700 SGT was 1900 EDT the previous day until 1 Nov 2026, and is 1800 EST from then on (US clocks went back on 1 Nov). (1 Nov, 5 Nov)
- **Global benchmark quotes stay in USD without a sourced same-day rate.** Brent and similar quotes have been given in USD when no same-day USD/SGD rate could be verified, rather than converted on a stale rate. Note the Brent contract month when the quoted level steps. (1 Oct, 31 Oct, 5 Nov)
- **A scheduled event is not an in-window event.** A conference, vote or launch dated after the window close is tomorrow's story, even when previews run inside the window; only the in-window preview material may be used, and then only if it carries substance. (25 Oct, 8-9 Nov)
- **Live FX endpoints are all blocked by the proxy** (api.data.gov.sg, open.er-api.com, api.frankfurter.app, xe.com): carry USD/SGD 1.2800 from state, and flag any cross-rate taken from a search summary as stale with its date. (24 Oct)
