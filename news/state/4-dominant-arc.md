# Section 4 — Dominant story arc: IRAN WAR (mutable; state as of 9 July 2026)

- **Origin:** 28 Feb 2026 — US/Israel large-scale strikes on Iran; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei assassinated. Iran closed Hormuz; missile/drone campaign across region.
- **MOU:** signed electronically June 17 (Trump at Versailles, Pezeshkian in Tehran). 14-point framework: 60-day ceasefire extension; Hormuz toll-free reopening; US naval blockade lifted; nuclear file deferred to Phase 2 (60-day window: enrichment moratorium, HEU removal, IAEA snap inspections). Four working groups in Switzerland: Sanctions Termination, Nuclear Affairs, Iran Reconstruction & Economic Development, Monitoring & Implementation. **9 Jul: Trump declared the ceasefire "over"** (no formal termination notice filed — a de facto abrogation) after the 7 Jul Hormuz tanker attacks and follow-on US strikes; ~22 of 60 MOU days elapsed. This is the most serious breach since signing; the MOU's practical status is now unclear pending mediator efforts.
- **8-9 Jul escalation:** Following the 7 Jul tanker attacks and CENTCOM's initial retaliatory strikes, Trump warned US action would "get much worse" ("Every time they hit us, we hit them 20") and CENTCOM conducted a further round of "additional strikes" against Iranian air-defence/coastal targets — Iranian state media reported explosions on Kharg Island (~90% of Iran's crude oil exports), Bandar Abbas, Sirik and Qeshm. Trump floated a possible US takeover of Kharg Island and reimposing a full blockade, on top of the 7 Jul revocation of Iran's oil-export waiver (wind-down deadline 17 Jul). Oil: Brent +5.2% to $78.02, WTI +4.4% to $73.52 — sharpest single-day move of the crisis so far, though still short of full war-premium pricing.
- **Diplomacy:** Doha indirect US-Iran technical talks July 1 — "positive progress"; talks paused for Khamenei funeral (4-9 Jul), agreed to resume "as soon as possible" after processions conclude — now complicated by the 7-8 Jul escalation. Pakistan and Qatar foreign ministers, Egypt's intelligence chief, Turkey's Erdogan and Saudi officials reported working around the clock to keep the MOU from collapsing outright as of 8-9 Jul.
- **Hormuz:** traffic (31–43 crossings/day 3-5 Jul, vs 120-140/day pre-war) fell further after the 7 Jul attacks. IMO: ~6,000 seafarers remain stranded in the Persian Gulf (9 Jul); the broader ~11,000-seafarer evacuation plan remains paused after a Singapore-flagged container ship was attacked near Oman in late June. Iran has mined large stretches of the strait and, per US officials, may lack the capability to locate/clear all mines itself. Brent/WTI above. Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd still on Cape routing. ADNOC: full flows not before 2027.
- **Lebanon:** MOU calls for end of hostilities on all fronts incl. Lebanon. Israel not party; Netanyahu says forces stay "indefinitely." Hezbollah not party. No new Lebanon-specific development 7-9 Jul; remains the primary secondary tripwire alongside Hormuz.
- **Nuclear:** Iran holds 440.9kg HEU at 60%. IAEA access dispute unresolved as of 9 Jul — Iran's deputy FM (7 Jul) said access will be "reviewed and decided only within the framework of a final agreement" tied to full sanctions relief. No further movement reported 8-9 Jul; Phase 2 talks paused for the funeral and now further complicated by the ceasefire's practical collapse.
- **Khamenei funeral and succession:** Najaf procession 8 Jul drew an estimated 2.3 million mourners (Iraq PMF), continuing to Karbala the same day; burial at the Imam Reza shrine in Mashhad due 9 Jul. **New Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei did not attend the Iraq processions** and was reportedly advised against travelling to Mashhad over fears of being targeted by Israel — his continued public absence (tied to leg/facial injuries from the war's first day) remains the single biggest unresolved signal of who is actually directing Iran's response during the current escalation.
- **Positions:** US — Trump's rhetoric has hardened sharply (ceasefire "over," threats to take Kharg Island); pre-midterm calendar still a constraint but less visibly restraining now. Iran — foreign ministry/mediators reportedly still want the MOU held; IRGC acting with wide unilateral latitude; Mojtaba Khamenei's continued absence is the key open question. Israel — not bound, continuing Lebanon ops. Pakistan/Qatar/Egypt/Turkey/Saudi — active crisis mediation as of 8-9 Jul, distinct from the earlier slower-paced working-group diplomacy. China — bilateral Hormuz transit arrangements; no confirmed military support to Iran.
- **Open questions:** Does "ceasefire over" mean the MOU is functionally dead, or is this brinkmanship ahead of a mediated re-stabilisation? Does the US actually move on Kharg Island? Does Mojtaba Khamenei reappear publicly once the funeral period ends, and does that change Iran's posture? Phase 2 nuclear timeline now looks very unlikely to hold within its original 60-day window.
