## Background (permanent; keep short)
- **Origin:** 28 Feb 2026 — US/Israel large-scale strikes on Iran; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei assassinated. Iran closed Hormuz; missile/drone campaign across region.
- **MOU:** signed electronically June 17 (Trump at Versailles, Pezeshkian in Tehran). 14-point framework: 60-day ceasefire extension; Hormuz toll-free reopening; US naval blockade lifted; nuclear file deferred to Phase 2. Four working groups in Switzerland. **9 Jul: Trump declared the ceasefire "over"** after the 7 Jul Hormuz tanker attacks; the MOU's practical status has now deteriorated further with two consecutive nights of large-scale US strikes (8-9 Jul) and Iranian retaliation against four additional states (Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan) on 9 Jul. No formal termination notice has been filed by either side.
