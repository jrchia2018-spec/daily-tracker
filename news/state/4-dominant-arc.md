# Section 4 — Dominant story arc: IRAN WAR (mutable; state as of 6 July 2026)

- **Origin:** 28 Feb 2026 — US/Israel large-scale strikes on Iran; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei assassinated. Iran closed Hormuz; missile/drone campaign across region.
- **MOU:** signed electronically June 17 (Trump at Versailles, Pezeshkian in Tehran). 14-point framework: 60-day ceasefire extension; Hormuz toll-free reopening; US naval blockade lifted; nuclear file deferred to Phase 2 (60-day window: enrichment moratorium, HEU removal, IAEA snap inspections). Four working groups in Switzerland: Sanctions Termination, Nuclear Affairs, Iran Reconstruction & Economic Development, Monitoring & Implementation. ~19 of 60 MOU days elapsed as of 6 July.
- **Diplomacy:** Doha indirect US-Iran technical talks July 1 — "positive progress"; $6B frozen assets partly unfrozen (for Iranian goods purchases); Qatar/Pakistan reporting. Talks suspended for Khamenei state funeral July 4–9; resume earliest after processions.
- **Hormuz:** contested but slowly reopening. Traffic +50% week of June 22–28 (Kpler) — still ~5 vessels/day vs 93 pre-crisis at last hard count. IRGC re-declared closed June 20 citing Israel's Lebanon ops; US denied; CENTCOM reported 55 transits June 20. PGSA still asserting routing authority; foreign-flagged container ship ran aground July 1 after deviating from Iran's corridor. US Navy JMIC widened alternative route near Oman June 27. Central channel mined (~80 mines, 40–50 days to clear). Brent ~$73 — war premium unwound. Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd still on Cape routing. ADNOC: full flows not before 2027.
- **Lebanon:** MOU calls for end of hostilities on all fronts incl. Lebanon. Israel not party; Netanyahu says forces stay "indefinitely." Hezbollah not party. Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire renewed June 19, violated within 24h. Iran treats Israeli Lebanon strikes as MOU breach — primary tripwire for collapse.
- **Nuclear:** Iran holds 440.9kg HEU at 60%. Committed not to acquire nuclear weapons (JCPOA-style), no enforcement mechanism yet. Natanz, Fordow, Isfahan previously struck. HEU removal logistics studied by Witkoff and Kushner at Tennessee lab. Phase 2 60-day window active. **IAEA access dispute active:** Grossi says inspectors must access sites; parliament speaker Ghalibaf denies IAEA access to Fordow/Natanz/Isfahan. Core Phase 2 blocker.
- **Escalation signals:** Israeli DM Katz (July 1): Mojtaba Khamenei "marked for death"; Araghchi warned of "immediate powerful response." USS Boxer ARG (Boxer, Portland, Comstock; 11th MEU, 2,000+ Marines) deployed to Middle East as "scheduled deployment."
- **Positions:** US — war end, Hormuz open, dismantlement; Trump vacillating; pre-midterm calendar constrains strikes. Iran — foreign ministry wants MOU held; IRGC asserting Hormuz leverage; internal split; Mojtaba Khamenei's silence is the key signal. Israel — not bound, continuing Lebanon ops, Netanyahu under pressure. Pakistan — primary mediator, hosts working-group logistics. Gulf — want deal; Qatar/Pakistan co-mediate. China — bilateral Hormuz transit arrangements; no confirmed military support to Iran.
- **Open questions:** Lebanon clause collapsing MOU? IRGC Hormuz declarations hardening into enforcement? Phase 2 agreement within 60 days (vs 2-year historical precedent)? Trump resuming strikes pre-midterms if talks stall? IAEA site access resolved before Phase 2 substance can start?
