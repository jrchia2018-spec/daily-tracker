# Section 4 — Dominant story arc: IRAN WAR (mutable; state as of 8 July 2026)

- **Origin:** 28 Feb 2026 — US/Israel large-scale strikes on Iran; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei assassinated. Iran closed Hormuz; missile/drone campaign across region.
- **MOU:** signed electronically June 17 (Trump at Versailles, Pezeshkian in Tehran). 14-point framework: 60-day ceasefire extension; Hormuz toll-free reopening; US naval blockade lifted; nuclear file deferred to Phase 2 (60-day window: enrichment moratorium, HEU removal, IAEA snap inspections). Four working groups in Switzerland: Sanctions Termination, Nuclear Affairs, Iran Reconstruction & Economic Development, Monitoring & Implementation. ~21 of 60 MOU days elapsed as of 8 July — ceasefire now visibly fracturing rather than consolidating (see Escalation below).
- **7 July escalation (major):** Projectiles/drone hit three commercial vessels in Hormuz (Qatari LNG carrier Al Rekayyat off Limah, Oman — fire, crew safe; a Saudi-flagged crude tanker; a third vessel by drone) — first shipping attack since June 29. CENTCOM responded with "a series of powerful strikes" on Iranian air-defence, coastal-surveillance, SAM, anti-ship-missile and drone-launch sites and port facilities near Bandar Abbas, Qeshm Island and Sirik (Air Force/Navy tactical aircraft). US Treasury same day revoked the June oil-export waiver; wind-down deadline 17 July. Iran's military called the US response a "miscalculation," warned of "decisive and swift response" to further US interference. This is the most serious MOU breach since signing.
- **Diplomacy:** Doha indirect US-Iran technical talks July 1 — "positive progress"; $6B frozen assets partly unfrozen (for Iranian goods purchases); Qatar/Pakistan reporting. Talks suspended for Khamenei state funeral July 4–9; no confirmed resumption date yet as of 8 July, and the 7 July escalation makes near-term resumption less likely.
- **Hormuz:** was slowly reopening (Kpler: 31–43 crossings/day July 3–5, vs 120–140/day pre-war — a lower baseline than the ~93/day figure cited in late June, per Kpler's 7 Jul methodology) before the 7 July attacks reversed the trend; traffic fell sharply again after the strikes. PGSA still asserting routing authority; foreign-flagged container ship ran aground July 1 after deviating from Iran's corridor. US Navy JMIC widened alternative route near Oman June 27. Central channel mined (~80 mines, 40–50 days to clear). Brent $72.85 (+2.6% on 7 Jul attacks) — still near 4-month lows, war premium largely unpriced. Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd still on Cape routing. ADNOC: full flows not before 2027.
- **Lebanon:** MOU calls for end of hostilities on all fronts incl. Lebanon. Israel not party; Netanyahu says forces stay "indefinitely." Hezbollah not party. Israel-Hezbollah ceasefire renewed June 19, violated within 24h. Iran treats Israeli Lebanon strikes as MOU breach — primary tripwire for collapse (no new Lebanon-specific development 7-8 Jul).
- **Nuclear:** Iran holds 440.9kg HEU at 60%. Committed not to acquire nuclear weapons (JCPOA-style), no enforcement mechanism yet. Natanz, Fordow, Isfahan previously struck. HEU removal logistics studied by Witkoff and Kushner at Tennessee lab. Phase 2 60-day window active. **IAEA access dispute active and hardening:** Grossi says inspectors must access sites; parliament speaker Ghalibaf denies IAEA access to Fordow/Natanz/Isfahan. As of 7 Jul, an Iranian deputy FM went further, saying access will be "reviewed and decided only within the framework of a final agreement" tied to full sanctions relief — access now explicitly conditional, not just denied. Core Phase 2 blocker.
- **Escalation signals:** Israeli DM Katz (July 1): Mojtaba Khamenei "marked for death"; Araghchi warned of "immediate powerful response." USS Boxer ARG (Boxer, Portland, Comstock; 11th MEU, 2,000+ Marines) deployed to Middle East as "scheduled deployment." 7 Jul: Hormuz tanker attacks + US strikes + oil waiver revocation (above) — the most concrete escalation signal yet.
- **Khamenei funeral:** Tehran procession July 6 (FT est. 12–15M mourners); Qom ceremonies July 7 (6am, Jamkaran Mosque, prayers led by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli); Najaf and Karbala (Iraq) processions due July 8; burial in Mashhad to follow. Mojtaba Khamenei's public silence through the funeral period continues to be the key unresolved succession signal.
- **Positions:** US — war end, Hormuz open, dismantlement; Trump vacillating; pre-midterm calendar constrains strikes; oil-waiver revocation shows willingness to use economic levers. Iran — foreign ministry wants MOU held; IRGC asserting Hormuz leverage and now acting on it; internal split; Mojtaba Khamenei's silence is the key signal. Israel — not bound, continuing Lebanon ops, Netanyahu under pressure. Pakistan — primary mediator, hosts working-group logistics. Gulf — want deal; Qatar/Pakistan co-mediate; Qatar and Saudi Arabia now directly victims of the 7 Jul tanker attacks. China — bilateral Hormuz transit arrangements; no confirmed military support to Iran.
- **Open questions:** Does the 7 Jul escalation collapse the MOU outright, or stay contained as a one-off tit-for-tat? Lebanon clause collapsing MOU separately? Phase 2 agreement within 60 days (vs 2-year historical precedent) — now looking less likely given IAEA hardening? Trump resuming broader strikes pre-midterms if talks stall? Does revoking the oil waiver push Iran toward or away from cooperation?
